# VCE Flour Alternatives

[日本語](README.md) | **English**

A mod that lets rice and corn flour substitute for wheat flour in Vanilla Cooking Expanded (VCE) baked dishes. It adds no new dishes or milling equipment.

## Requirements and installation

Requires RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7, Harmony, Vanilla Expanded Framework and Vanilla Cooking Expanded. Load Harmony before Core, then Framework, VCE and this mod.

Extract the mod ZIP from [Releases](https://github.com/pengin0503/rimworld-VCE-Flour-Patch/releases), place the mod folder in RimWorld's `Mods` folder and enable it. Back up saves before changing mods.

## Features

- Make rice and corn flour at fueled stoves, electric stoves or crafting spots. Bills convert 20 ingredients into 20 flour or 80 into 80. Crafting spots work at half speed.
- Seven standard VCE baking recipes accept one of the three flour types. Multiple stacks of one type may be combined, but different flours may not be mixed. Recipes from other mods such as VCE Bakery are excluded.
- Existing milling bills migrate to the 20-unit bill. Add a new bill for 80 units. The new flours have the same nutrition as wheat flour.

## Validation

Successful in-game testing has been reported on 1.7. No separate in-game confirmation has been reported for 1.6. The latest changes to milling work and workstations have not been verified in-game.
